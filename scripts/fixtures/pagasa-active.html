TROPICAL CYCLONE BULLETIN NO. 12

Typhoon «AGHON» (MAN-YI)

Issued at 5:00 PM, 25 September 2026

Valid for broadcast until the next bulletin at 11:00 PM today.

TROPICAL CYCLONE WIND SIGNAL NO. 3

Luzon: the northern portion of Ilocos Norte (Pagudpud, Burgos, Bangui, Adams, Dumalneg, Vintar) and the northwestern portion of Cagayan

TROPICAL CYCLONE WIND SIGNAL NO. 2

Luzon: the rest of Ilocos Norte, Ilocos Sur, Abra, Apayao, and the northern portion of La Union (Bangar, Sudipen, Balaoan)

TROPICAL CYCLONE WIND SIGNAL NO. 1

Luzon: the rest of La Union, Pangasinan, Benguet, Ifugao, Mountain Province, Kalinga, Nueva Vizcaya, and the rest of Cagayan

LOCATION OF CENTER

The center of the eye of Typhoon «AGHON» was estimated at 160 km West of Laoag City, Ilocos Norte.

INTENSITY

Maximum sustained winds of 150 km/h near the center, gustiness of up to 185 km/h, and central pressure of 960 hPa.

MOVEMENT

West Northwestward at 20 km/h.
